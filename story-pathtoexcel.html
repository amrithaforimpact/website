Helping 35,000 children learn through play

At PathToExcel, I led curriculum and product for a game-based learning platform for children in kindergarten through third grade.

We built more than 100 hours of standards-aligned lessons and worked directly with school districts to bring the platform into classrooms.

The platform reached more than 35,000 learners across five districts, and enrollment grew 25%.
